I made the text centred on my card to keep it from looking too asymmetrical and ugly, and I chose a slimy-looking font to fit its lizardy (is "lizardy" a word?) theme. I also wanted the background of my card to be green (like many reptiles), so I used MSPaint for the front's background and a CSS linear gradient for the back. I matched the text colour to the background, and I used a contrast-checking website to make sure it would be readable for most people. As a reference to the old box model being out-of-date, I tried to make the graphic design look similar to quite a few fun-looking old websites.
