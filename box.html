I made the text centred on my card to keep it from extending off of the sides or looking too asymmetrical, and I chose a pretty slimy-looking font to fit its theme. I also wanted the background of my card to be green, since lots of snakes and lizards are green, so I used MSPaint for the front's background and a CSS linear gradient for the back. I matched the text colour to the background, and I used the contrast-checking website to make sure it worked.
